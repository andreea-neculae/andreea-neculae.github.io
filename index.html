About

Andreea Neculae is a real estate writer and data journalist who covers the flexible workspace industry and commercial real estate. She is a writer and market analyst at CoworkingCafe and CoworkingMag, both divisions of Yardi Systems, where she produces market studies on coworking pricing, market growth, amenities and the shifts changing where people work.

She has written for CoworkingCafe and CoworkingMag since 2023 and also contributes to WorkCafe. She previously wrote for RentCafe, starting in 2021. Her research has been cited by The Business Journals, The New York Times and Forbes. She holds an academic background in Language Arts and writes in both English and Romanian.

Outside her reporting work, she writes fiction and book reviews.
