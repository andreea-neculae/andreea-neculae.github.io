About

Andreea Neculae is a real estate writer and data journalist who covers the flexible workspace industry and commercial real estate.

  • Writer and data journalist, CoworkingCafe and CoworkingMag
  • Contributor to WorkCafe
  • Previously wrote for RentCafe, from 2021
  • Writes in English and Romanian

At CoworkingCafe and CoworkingMag, both divisions of Yardi Systems, she produces market studies on coworking pricing, market growth, amenities and the shifts changing where people work. She has written for both since 2023.

Her research has been cited by The Business Journals, The New York Times and Forbes. She holds an academic background in Language Arts. Outside her reporting work, she writes fiction and book reviews.
